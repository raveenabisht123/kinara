    How a visit works

    Care that starts with listening.

    A first consultation is booked long enough to hear the whole story. Then we keep it on file.

    1. Book in under a minute

      Choose a service and a preferred day. We confirm a slot on WhatsApp.

    2. Unhurried first consult

      Your doctor takes a full history once. It goes into one record shared by all five departments.

    3. Tests in the same building

      If you need bloodwork or a scan, the diagnostics team is downstairs. Reports reach your phone.

    4. A follow-up that's planned

      You leave with a written plan and a follow-up date, not a vague "come back if it continues".

    Health information

    Know your numbers.

    Common reference ranges for healthy adults. Your doctor reads them against your age, history and medicines, so use these as a guide, not a diagnosis.

    MeasureHealthy rangeWhat it tells you
    Blood pressureBelow 120/80 mmHg130/80 or higher on repeat readings is high blood pressure
    Fasting blood sugar70 to 99 mg/dL100 to 125 suggests prediabetes; 126 or higher, diabetes
    HbA1cBelow 5.7%Average sugar over 3 months; 6.5% or higher indicates diabetes
    BMI (Asian adults)18.5 to 22.923 to 24.9 is overweight for Indians; 25 or more, obese
    Waist sizeMen under 90 cm, women under 80 cmBelly fat raises heart and diabetes risk even at a normal BMI
    LDL cholesterolBelow 100 mg/dLLower targets apply if you have diabetes or heart disease
    HaemoglobinMen 13 to 17, women 12 to 15 g/dLLow values suggest anaemia, common in Indian women and children
    Vitamin D30 to 100 ng/mLBelow 20 is deficient; very common even in sunny cities
    Go to emergency, call 112

    Don't book a slot for these

    • Chest pain or pressure, especially spreading to the arm or jaw
    • Sudden weakness of face, arm or leg, or slurred speech
    • Severe breathlessness, or lips turning blue
    • Fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or more in a baby under 3 months
    • A seizure, fainting, or heavy bleeding that won't stop

    Book a visit within 24 hours for

    • Fever lasting more than 3 days
    • A cough beyond 2 weeks, or with blood
    • A child who is drinking less or passing less urine
    • A new rash with fever, or a mole that has changed
    Questions

    Before you book.

    Can I get a same-day appointment?

    Usually, yes. Book the form below and we'll offer the earliest open slot on WhatsApp. For a fever or a child who is unwell, mention it in the form and we prioritise the slot.

    Do I pay anything to book?

    No. Booking is free. You pay the consultation fee at the clinic, by UPI, card or cash.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Reply to the WhatsApp confirmation at any time. There's no cancellation charge.

    Do you accept health insurance?

    Cashless OPD is available with most major insurers and TPAs. If yours is not on the list, we give you an itemised bill for reimbursement.

    Can I book for my child or a parent?

    Yes. Enter their name in the form and your phone number. Each family member gets their own record, linked to yours.

    Is this for emergencies?

    No. For chest pain, breathing trouble, heavy bleeding or an accident, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
